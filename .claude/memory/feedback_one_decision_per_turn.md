---
name: Present several decisions one per turn
description: When guiding Frank through more than one open decision in conversation, put one decision per turn and write the answers up together once they are all in.
type: feedback
---

When a conversation leaves Frank with several decisions to make, present them one per turn. Explain each one starting from what happens today, then name the options. Collect the answers as they come and write them all up together once the last one is in — never deliver the whole set in one message for him to work through.

**Why:** Six open items delivered in one message on #268 went undecided; stepped through one at a time, every item was settled and two recommendations were reversed. The first of them was explained further before being answered, which a batched message leaves no room for. The same approach then settled the seven decisions behind #331.

**How to apply:** One decision per message, in plain consequences before any mechanism (CLAUDE.md: "Don't frame a decision in implementation jargon"). See [[feedback_plain_language_decisions]] for how to put a quantified cost, and [[feedback_decisiveness_over_hedging]] for whether the choice is genuinely open enough to put at all.
